05 · About the study

A model for reflection,
not a formula to apply.

This web app illustrates the conceptual model proposed by Thang Dinh Truong and Philip Hallinger. Their study synthesizes qualitative data from three schools in Vietnam and suggests that effective leadership in this context combines legitimate authority with moral prestige.

The indicators in this app are designed only to help users observe relationships among the model's components. They are not empirical measures, a diagnostic tool, or a prescriptive recommendation.
